Adds a sponge for soaking up water in a configurable distance from the selected block. Especially nice for translocators which spawn in water, which are impossible to get dry.

  • Crafted with a 9 cloth
  • Right click with dry liquid sponge to absorb water
  • Right click to wring out wet liquid sponge (will not deposit any water)
  • Let wet liquid sponge dry out over time (through perishing / "rotting" mechanic)
