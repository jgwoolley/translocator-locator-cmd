Adds the ability to repair an unrepairable translocator. Within Better Ruins this form of translocator is used a lot, so it will be particularly useful there!

  • Right click an unrepairable translocator with cupronickel rods in your hand: 3 rods, or just 1 if you are a Clockmaker
  • The translocator becomes a normal broken translocator, which you then repair with temporal gears as usual
  • Hovering over an unrepairable translocator shows that it is missing a cupronickel rod, and the interaction help lists the rods needed
  • Works as a block behavior added to the translocator, so it does not replace the block class and should not conflict with other mods that change translocators
  • Required on both client and server
