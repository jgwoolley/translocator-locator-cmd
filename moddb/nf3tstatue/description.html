Carve a stone statue of a player, or cast one in metal. Pick a player and a pose, and the statue shows that player frozen in the moment, carved from the stone you used.

Making a statue

Craft a statue block from stone bricks of one rock type. Marble statues come from polished marble.

  • Small (about one block tall): four bricks in a 2x2
  • Large (the size of the Tobias statue, filling a 3x3 area): nine bricks in a 3x3
  • Bust (just the head): two bricks in a row

Place the block, hold a chisel in your hand and a hammer in your off hand, and right-click it to open the design dialog. When you press Commit, the block becomes the finished statue. It can't be carved again, but it keeps its design when you break it and place it somewhere else.

Designing it

Choose a player who is online and a pose: wave, cheer, shrug, cry, nod, facepalm, bow, laugh, rage, standing or riding. A turning preview plays the pose; pick any frame, or let Best frame find the most expressive one.

The statue copies what the player wears, in three layers you can switch on or off: hair, face and beard; clothing; armor. Each hand can hold a copy of an item from your hotbar, carved in the same stone. Nothing is taken from you.

The < and > buttons turn the statue, and the compass direction it faces is shown between them. You can also remove the pedestal so it stands right on the ground. Copy design from fills in every setting from a finished statue in your hotbar.

Riding an elk

Put the player on a stone elk, a cow or an antlered bull, standing still, walking or galloping, with bridle, face mask, saddle and saddlebags if you like. Pick a nearby elk to copy its look, or set it by hand.

Metal statues

Metal is cast, not chiseled. Pack fire clay around a finished stone statue until it is buried, then lift off the mold; the stone statue stays as it was. Place the mold, pour molten metal into the hole on top, and break the mold to get the statue in gold, silver, copper, the bronzes and more.

Special statues

Besides players there is a retro tin robot, with a stalk neck, a chest dial and pincer claws. It is carved, copied and cast like any other statue.

Good to know

The player has to be online when the statue is carved. Required on both client and server.
