Adds commands to center the map on a coordinate and mark it with a temporary marker that disappears when the map is closed, plus shareable chat links for locations.

  • .panmap <x> <y> <z> [name] centers the map if it is already open and marks the spot. If the map is closed it gives you a link to open it at that location.
  • .panmapshare [x y z] [name] shares a location (your own position if you give no coordinates) with everyone on the server as a clickable chat link.

Coordinates are relative to the world spawn, like the map and the coordinate arguments of commands. The height is the real height.

Works client-only. If the mod is also on the server, every player who has it gets the shared-location links; players without the mod see nothing. Sharing is limited to once every two seconds per player.
