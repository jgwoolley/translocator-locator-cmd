Adds the temporal loom, a Jonas device (1 block wide, 1.5 tall) powered by temporal gears. It weaves flax twine into glowing blue-green temporal fiber, which is crafted into a temporal sewing kit that weaves time back into ruined (rotten) tapestries.

  • Right-click the loom with a temporal gear to power it (16 charges per gear), with flax twine to load it, and with an empty hand to take the temporal fiber
  • It weaves one temporal fiber from one flax twine and one charge every 3 seconds
  • Craft 1 temporal fiber and 1 stick into a temporal sewing kit (fiber over stick)
  • Hold the sewing kit and right-click a ruined tapestry to restore it; each repair uses 1 of the kit's 3 uses (nothing is used in creative mode)
  • The loom is crafted in the grid from Jonas parts and a temporal gear: spring frame, temporal gear, Jonas cylinder / cupronickel rod, gears frame, cupronickel rod / lead plate, metal parts, lead plate
  • Hovering over a ruined tapestry shows the repair hint, and the interaction help shows the kit
  • Only ruined tapestries are affected; everything else about tapestries is unchanged
  • The unraveling loom is its inverse: it runs on rusty gears and unravels flax twine into decayed fiber. Craft 1 decayed fiber with 1 stick into a temporal decay kit (3 uses) and right-click a tapestry to make it rotten. The loom is crafted like the temporal loom, with a rusty gear instead of the temporal gear
  • Required on both client and server
