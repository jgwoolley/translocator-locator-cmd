# Reliquiarum

*Some things in this world were never made here.*

Far from any road, under moss and rust and drifted soil, lie things that do not belong. Metal that no smith of this world
poured. Gold with a face nobody remembers. Machines that stopped watching a very long time ago. Nobody knows who left them,
where they came from, or how they came to rest here.

Reliquiarum scatters these relics across the map. They are rare, and nothing points the way: no map marker, no handbook
entry, no recipe, no trader who has heard of them. You will not find one by looking for it. You will find one because you
walked somewhere nobody had a reason to go.

What you make of it is up to you. Leave it where it lies, build a shrine around it, or carry it home and wonder.

## What to expect

- **Treasures from other worlds.** Each relic is a single, strange object: no two finds tell the same story, and none of
  them explains itself.
- **Nothing is given away.** The relics are kept out of the handbook on purpose. This page does not list them either.
- **Nothing to grind.** No new ores, tools or progression. The find is the reward.

## Status

Test build (0.1.0), not on the ModDB yet. **Only a few relics are hidden in the world so far**: the rest can only be
placed from the creative inventory, in their own *Reliquiarum* tab, so builders and testers can look at them. Scattering
them all through worldgen is the next step. New worlds only: worldgen does not reach land that is already generated.

Requires Vintage Story 1.22.7, on the server and on every client.
